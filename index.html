Your decisions follow the work

From the session to the PR to long after merge, temory keeps the work tied to what your team decided.

01In the session

Changes come to you

When a teammate changes a decision, moves a ticket or edits a wiki page, you see it in your session at your next prompt, with the source. If a request breaks a current decision, your agent stops and asks first.

NO TEMORY SERVER

Your team's data stays on your machine. temory has no server. Extraction runs through your own Claude Code or Codex, under your plan's existing data terms.

READ-ONLY

Only reads your tools

temory reads with the access you already have. It never posts, edits or deletes anything in them.
